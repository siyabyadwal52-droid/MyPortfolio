Introduction

Beginning the Engineering Journey

Curious, grounded, and eager to discover how tiny silicon wafers power the world.

Hello! I am Siya Byadwal, a first-year student pursuing my Bachelor of Technology in Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) at JECRC University, Jaipur.

I am at the very start of my technical journey. As a beginner, I believe that genuine engineering begins with asking fundamental questions and building conceptual clarity. Rather than rushing into advanced claims, I am spending my first year understanding core mathematics, circuit analysis, electronic components, and basic computer programming.

What truly excites me about ECE is the realm of Semiconductor Technology and Chip Design. The idea that billions of nanoscale transistors can be precisely laid out on a silicon chip to execute complex logic is both awe-inspiring and deeply inspiring. My goal is to dedicate my college years to learning the physics, logic, and design methodologies that make this possible.

“I am here to learn, experiment, make mistakes in the lab, and grow steadily into a competent hardware and semiconductor engineer.”

Genuine Curiosity

Eager to understand the physical intuition behind Ohm's law, semiconductor bandgaps, and logic gates rather than just memorizing formulas.

Semiconductor Interest

Fascinated by VLSI, microelectronics, and how silicon wafer fabrication enables high-performance computing hardware.

Hands-on Exploration

Looking forward to breadboard experimentation, circuit simulation tools, microcontroller prototyping, and programming practice.
